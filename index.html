TUMBANG PRESOlaro sa kalye, bago tawagin ni Nanay

Ibato ang tsinelas, patumbahin ang lata, at kunin ito pabalik bago ka mahuli ng taya. Kapag nahuli ka, ikaw na ang taya!

🎮 Works with a PlayStation or any controller · keyboard · touch

Paano laruin

  1. Behind the chalk line you are safe. Hold to aim at the lata, let go when the power is right, and your tsinelas flies.
  2. Knock the can down and the taya must stand it back in its circle before tagging anyone. That's your chance to fetch your slipper and run home.
  3. Out past the line you can be tagged, unless you are standing on your own tsinelas. Wait for the right moment!
  4. Tagged? You're the taya now: guard the can and chase whoever steps out. Tag someone to get back to throwing.
  5. Play until 6:00, when Nanay calls everyone home to eat.
